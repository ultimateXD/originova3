Everything you need to know about luxury travel with ORIGINOVA

Our luxury packages include premium accommodations at 5-star hotels, private transportation with professional drivers, expert Egyptologist guides, all entrance fees to attractions, gourmet meals at select restaurants, VIP airport services with fast-track immigration, and 24/7 concierge support. Specific inclusions vary by package - view full details in each itinerary.

Cancellations made 60+ days before departure receive a full refund minus a $200 processing fee. Cancellations 30-59 days prior receive 50% refund. Cancellations within 30 days are non-refundable. We strongly recommend comprehensive travel insurance. Emergency cancellations due to medical reasons or travel advisories are reviewed case-by-case.

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), bank wire transfers, and PayPal. A 30% deposit is required to confirm your reservation, with the balance due 45 days before departure. For bookings within 45 days of travel, full payment is required. We offer flexible payment plans for packages over $5,000.

Yes, all packages include luxury airport transfers with meet-and-greet service. Our representative will be waiting at arrivals with a personalized sign. VIP fast-track immigration and lounge access are available as upgrades. All ground transportation uses premium vehicles (Mercedes S-Class or BMW 7 Series) with professional English-speaking drivers.

Yes, we provide comprehensive visa assistance. Most nationalities can obtain an Egypt e-Visa online or visa-on-arrival at Cairo Airport. We'll guide you through the process and provide all necessary documentation. Our team also advises on passport validity requirements (6 months minimum), travel insurance, and health requirements. Visa fees are not included in package prices.

Absolutely! All our itineraries are fully customizable. Want to add extra days in Luxor? Prefer a different hotel? Need dietary accommodations? Our travel designers will tailor every aspect to your preferences. We specialize in creating bespoke luxury experiences - from private museum after-hours tours to exclusive desert camps. Contact us to design your perfect Egyptian journey.

October to April offers the most comfortable weather for sightseeing, with temperatures ranging from 20-30°C (68-86°F). December to February is peak season with cooler evenings. Summer (May-September) is hot but less crowded, ideal for Red Sea beach destinations. We recommend booking 3-6 months in advance for peak season travel to secure the best hotels and guides.

Yes, Egypt's major tourist destinations are very safe, especially for luxury travelers with private guides and transportation.
